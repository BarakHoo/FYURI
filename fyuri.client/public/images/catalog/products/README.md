# Generated catalogue product art

These WebP assets are AI-generated catalogue illustrations created for the
FYURI review branch on 2026-07-31. They are representative marketing visuals,
not official manufacturer photography, dimensional drawings, or authoritative
product specifications.

The shared art direction is a clean three-quarter product view on a deep
midnight-navy studio background, with matte-black materials, controlled cyan
rim lighting, restrained blue optical reflections, and no embedded text,
branding, watermarks, people, or weapons.

The SKU-to-asset assignments live in
`src/data/productImages.js`. Product pages use these illustrations first and
retain backend imagery as a fallback/gallery source.
